Privacy Policy

Glowfill, by RossBoyGames — last updated October 2026

What we collect

Glowfill has no account or sign-up — we don't collect your name, email, or any personal information directly. Your coins, streak, solved shapes, level progress, and settings are stored on your device. The only gameplay information that leaves your device is the anonymous analytics described under "Analytics" below.

Advertising

Glowfill uses Google AdMob to show occasional interstitial ads between rounds. AdMob may collect device and advertising identifiers to serve and measure ads. Before any cross-app tracking, iOS will ask your permission via the App Tracking Transparency prompt — if you decline, ads may still show, just not personalized ones. See Google's Privacy Policy for how AdMob handles data.

Analytics

Glowfill uses GameAnalytics, a third-party analytics service, to understand how the game is played so we can improve it. GameAnalytics receives:

  • A device identifier — a random, anonymous ID the game generates and stores on your device. It is not your name, your email, or your iOS advertising identifier.
  • Gameplay and progression events — for example when you start, complete, or fail a level, with details such as score, stars, and time.
  • Purchase events — which item was bought, and its price and currency. Payment details are handled by Apple and are never seen by us or by GameAnalytics.
  • Ad events — whether an ad was shown, clicked, or failed to load.
  • Basic device and app information — such as device type, iOS version, app version, and connection type. GameAnalytics may also derive your approximate country from your IP address.

This data is used only for analytics, to improve the game. It is not used for advertising or tracking: we don't combine it with advertising identifiers, and we don't use it to target ads or share it with advertisers. See GameAnalytics' Privacy Policy for how they handle data.

Your choices

EU and UK. If you're in the EU or UK, the first time you open the game you'll see a consent form (provided by Google) asking whether we may use your data for ads and analytics. Analytics stays off until you consent, and stays off if you decline. You can change your choice at any time with Privacy Settings in the game (in the Shop tab), which reopens the form.

Tracking on iOS. iOS will also ask whether the game may track your activity across other companies' apps and websites (Apple's tracking prompt). You can allow or deny it there, and change it any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Purchases

Optional purchases — Remove Ads and Power Packs of helpers — are processed entirely through Apple's App Store. We never see or store your payment information.

Children

Glowfill is a general-audience app and is not directed at children under 13.

Data we don't do

We don't sell your data, and we don't run analytics beyond what's described above.
