Common questions

I can't restore my Remove Ads or Power Pack purchase.
Open the Shop tab and tap "Restore purchases" while signed into the same Apple ID used for the original purchase.

My progress didn't carry over to a new device.
Glowfill's coins, streak, and level progress are stored locally on-device — they aren't currently synced across devices.

How do I get more coins or helpers?
Solve the daily puzzle and levels to earn coins, claim the daily reward on the home screen, or buy helper bundles and Power Packs in the Shop.
